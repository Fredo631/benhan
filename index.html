Terms of use

Last updated: 1 July 2026

By accessing or using the Service Victoria app, you agree to be bound by these terms of use. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

The Service Victoria app is provided by the State of Victoria. It offers a single sign-on for accessing participating Victorian government services, as well as a digital wallet for approved cards and licences.

Your responsibilities

You are responsible for keeping your sign-in details secure and for the accuracy of information you provide. Do not share your account with others.

Service availability

We aim to keep the app available at all times, but occasional downtime may occur. Critical services will always be available through alternative channels.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes indicates acceptance of the updated terms.

Privacy policy

Privacy policy

How we protect your information.

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We collect the information you provide when creating your account (name, date of birth, contact details) and usage data to improve the app.

How we use your information

Your information is used to verify your identity, provide access to services, and personalise your experience. We do not sell your data.

Data security

We use industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data. Your date of birth (26 April 2007) is stored securely and only shared with your consent.

Your rights

You can access, correct, or delete your personal information at any time through the app or by contacting us.
